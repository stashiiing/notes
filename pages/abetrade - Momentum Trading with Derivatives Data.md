- # Momentum Trading with Derivatives Data
- ## TL;DR
	- Momentum is buying strength or selling weakness and holding while the move continues. In crypto, moves travel further than they have any right to because of leverage and liquidation cascades. The hard part is not spotting the trend but distinguishing a real one from a trap. Open interest and funding are the primary tools for making that distinction. The healthy momentum setup has rising price, rising open interest, and muted or negative funding: new money committing, spot buyers in front, no froth.
- ### Context
	- Most trend-following strategies share the same return profile: many small losses punctuated by a handful of large winners. In crypto, the winners can be exceptionally large because moves are amplified by leverage and liquidation cascades. The derivatives data lets a trader filter out the setups most likely to reverse before they run.
- ## Core Concept
	- The three-variable check is the core of this approach. Rising price alone tells you nothing about whether the move has conviction. Rising open interest alongside it means new capital is committing rather than old positions rotating between hands. Muted or negative funding during the rally means the buying is coming from spot rather than from leveraged longs paying a premium to stay in. A perpetual trading at or below spot means spot buyers are out in front, dragging the perp up behind them.

	  The opposite combination (spiking open interest and sharply positive funding simultaneously) signals a crowded leveraged-long trade waiting to get flushed, which sets up a mean reversion trade rather than a momentum one.
- ## Entry / Trigger
	- Look for markets with spiking open interest alongside muted or negative funding, ideally breaking above or below a significant structural level in line with the moving averages.
	- A breakout above a key level with those conditions tends to trigger systematic strategies that pile in and fuel the move further.
	- Use technical analysis and indicators as the entry structure and invalidation point, with the derivatives data providing the directional bias.
- ## Risk Management
	- Momentum trades are built to be held. The return profile is many small losses and occasional large wins, so cutting trades early defeats the purpose.
	- Define a clear structural invalidation level at entry. If price breaks back through that level, the trend thesis is invalidated regardless of what open interest or funding show.
- ## Examples
	- PENGU (back half of 2025): Open interest sat flat around 40 to 50 million for months, then exploded past 150 million toward 290 million as the coin rallied. Funding stayed muted the entire way up and spiked hard negative in early July (to approximately minus 0.17%), meaning shorts were paying longs and the perp was trading below spot. Spot demand led, leveraged longs did not crowd in, and the rally ran far.
- ## Caveats & Edge Cases
	- Crypto is heavily correlated. Drawing precise support and resistance on an altcoin is usually pointless because most alts trade on their beta to BTC rather than their own chart. Keep technical analysis simple and use it as confluence rather than a standalone system.
	- Outliers that break away from BTC correlation exist but are rare. The derivatives data flags them by showing unusual open interest or funding patterns relative to the broader market.
