- # Sector Rotation Scan for Altcoin Selection
- ## TL;DR
	- In early bull trends, skew spends significant time screening sectors of the alt market for relative strength against BTC, ETH and SOL. With token dilution making manual chart-flipping impossible, he runs sector-level filters (e.g. AI sector, outperforming Bitcoin over 30/60/90 days), then narrows down to charts reclaiming key higher-timeframe structure. The goal is to catch obvious momentum trades that compound on top of his BTC core.
- ### Context
	- The generational-wealth returns in crypto come from alts, not BTC — projects with real fee generation, daily active users, and clear vertical upside (he names hype as a current example). But there are far too many tokens to track manually; without sector and relative-strength filtering, you can't even start.
- ## Core Concept
	- Trade where the strength is. A token that outperforms its benchmark (BTC, ETH, SOL) over multiple lookback windows is showing institutional or large-player accumulation. Combine that with a clean technical setup and you have an "easy" 2x–4x on leveraged size or a clean spot multi-bagger.
- ## Mechanics
	- **Step 1 — Sector identification.** Pick a sector of interest (AI, L1s, etc.). Filter for tokens that have outperformed Bitcoin over 30 / 60 / 90 days.
	- **Step 2 — Higher-timeframe structure check.** From the filtered list, chart each name. Look for:
		- Reclaim of a 1-week low, or
		- A higher high on a 1-week or 1-day basis (the 1D higher high is often the earliest signal of a regime transition).
	- **Step 3 — Confluence with EMAs.** Confirm by checking whether price has reclaimed the 4H 50 EMA and 4H 200 EMA. If both are reclaimed on a daily-trend chart, that's the entry signal.
	- **Step 4 — AI-assisted scanning (current evolution).** Skew mentions building scanning workflows with AI tools to surface ranked candidates faster than manual review allows.
- ## Entry / Trigger
	- Token shows multi-window outperformance vs BTC, has reclaimed a 1W low or made a 1D/1W higher high, and is trading above 4H 50 and 200 EMAs. That triple confirmation is the entry.
- ## Risk Management
	- Scale in: start at 1–5% of total portfolio per name, add as the trend confirms. Trailing stop on a moving average exits in profit when the trend breaks.
- ## Examples
	- The interview references hype as an example of a token with strong relative-strength characteristics and real fundamental backing (fee generation, real usage) that has attracted persistent investment.
- ## Caveats & Edge Cases
	- New tokens with no historical data don't fit this framework — they require narrative-based trading with a trailing stop instead.
	- Watch for unlocks and supply unlocks ("first-of-the-month" supply events) which can break a clean trend regardless of relative strength.
	- This is for early-bull-trend regimes. In ranges or downtrends, sector strength can flip quickly and the strategy underperforms.
