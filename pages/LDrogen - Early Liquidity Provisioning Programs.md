- # Early Liquidity Provisioning Programs
- ## TL;DR
	- Pre-launch DeFi protocols offer committed liquidity providers a combination of native protocol yield and token warrants struck at a fixed FDV valuation. The deal runs for 3-12 months. If the protocol gains traction and the TGE valuation exceeds the strike, the warrants add a significant additional APR to the trade. Starkiller treats this as the second largest source of alpha after diligence.
- ### Context
	- New protocols face a cold start problem: no liquidity, no users, no traction. To solve this, they approach institutional liquidity providers pre-launch with structured deals. For the liquidity provider, this offers access to above-market yields and token upside unavailable on the open market. The tradeoff is illiquidity and a VC-like bet on the protocol's success.
- ## Core Concept
	- The trade has two yield components: a native yield that is known in advance, and a contingent yield from token warrants that depends on TGE valuation. The native yield is evaluated like any other DeFi carry trade. The warrant component requires a VC-style assessment of whether the protocol will achieve or exceed the strike valuation at TGE.
- ## Mechanics
	- 1. Protocol approaches the fund pre-launch offering a liquidity commitment deal.
	- 2. Terms: commit a set amount (e.g. $500k-$5m) for a set period (e.g. 3, 6, 9, or 12 months).
	- 3. Native yield is paid throughout (e.g. 10% APR).
	- 4. Token warrants are issued struck at a fixed FDV at TGE (e.g. $125m FDV).
	- 5. If TGE valuation meets or exceeds the strike, the warrants add an additional APR equivalent (e.g. 15% additional APR on the capital committed).
	- 6. Evaluate the native yield on risk-adjusted basis as with any other DeFi opportunity.
	- 7. Separately evaluate the protocol's likelihood of achieving the TGE valuation: team, sector, backers, market conditions.
- ## Risk Management
	- The warrant component is an illiquid, binary-like bet; size accordingly.
	- The native yield component provides a floor return regardless of TGE outcome.
	- If the protocol fails to reach TGE valuation, the warrants expire worthless and the trade is evaluated on native yield alone.
- ## Examples
	- Indicative deal structure: $1m committed for 6 months, 10% native APR, warrants struck at $125m FDV. If TGE values the protocol at $150m or $300m, the total APR rises materially above the native rate.
- ## Caveats & Edge Cases
	- Requires genuine VC-style diligence on the protocol, not just yield sustainability analysis.
	- Lockup periods mean capital cannot be redeployed quickly if better opportunities arise.
	- TGE timing and valuation are outside the investor's control.
	- Token warrant value is highly sensitive to overall crypto market conditions at TGE.
