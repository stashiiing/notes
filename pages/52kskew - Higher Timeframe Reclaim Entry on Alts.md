- # Higher Timeframe Reclaim Entry on Alts
- ## TL;DR
	- The trade trigger is a higher-timeframe reclaim: a 1-week low taken back, or a 1-day / 1-week higher high formed, confirmed by price reclaiming both the 4H 50 EMA and the 4H 200 EMA. The 1D higher high is the earliest read on a trend transition; the EMA reclaim is the confirmation that the move has follow-through.
- ### Context
	- Most alt entries fail because the trader picks the level but not the *regime transition*. Skew's trigger is designed to catch the moment a higher-timeframe downtrend or range converts into the next higher-timeframe uptrend — that's when you get the bulk of the move with the least retrace risk.
- ## Core Concept
	- A reclaim of a major level on a higher timeframe is structurally different from a bounce within a range. The 1D higher high is usually the *first* technical evidence that the regime is shifting. The 4H EMA reclaim confirms the lower-timeframe trend has aligned with the higher-timeframe shift.
- ## Mechanics
	- **Identify the level.** On the 1W chart, find the most recent significant low. On the 1D, find the structure highs.
	- **Wait for one of two events.**
		- The 1W low is reclaimed (price back above a major weekly low after losing it), or
		- A 1D or 1W higher high prints, transitioning out of the prior trend.
	- **Confirm on the 4H.** Price must be trading above both the 4H 50 EMA and 4H 200 EMA.
	- **Enter.** "If it's reclaimed both, that's like, you know, I'm in essential."
- ## Entry / Trigger
	- Combined condition: 1W low reclaim *or* 1D/1W higher high, plus price > 4H 50 EMA and > 4H 200 EMA. Enter on confirmation of the EMA reclaim.
- ## Risk Management
	- Invalidation is loss of the EMA reclaim (price closes back below 4H 200 EMA) or a fresh 1D/1W lower low. Scale in at 1–5% of total portfolio if used as part of a multi-name basket; size larger if treating as a single high-conviction trade.
- ## Examples
	- Skew uses this trigger as the technical leg of his sector-rotation workflow: after the relative-strength filter surfaces a candidate, this is the chart pattern that turns it into a live trade.
- ## Caveats & Edge Cases
	- Works in early bull regimes. In a chop or range, EMA reclaims fail often and produce many false entries.
	- The 1D higher high is *earlier* than the 1W reclaim — earlier entries catch more of the move but with lower confirmation. Choose based on your risk appetite for the specific name.
