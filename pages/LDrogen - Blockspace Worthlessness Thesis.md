- # Blockspace Worthlessness Thesis
- ## TL;DR
	- Most L1 and L2 governance tokens are structurally worthless. The market was historically betting on blockspace as a proxy for the internet's value, the same way investors in the 1990s bought fibre optic companies as a proxy for the internet. When Ethereum launched blobs and blockspace became cheap and abundant, that proxy collapsed. Value now concentrates in protocols with genuine cash flows and direct token-business relationships.
- ### Context
	- For most of crypto's history, buying an L1 or L2 token was implicitly a bet that this blockchain would become the dominant settlement layer and capture enormous transaction fees. This "replacement heuristic" was intuitive but structurally flawed it priced blockspace as if scarcity would persist. The launch of Ethereum blobs changed the supply equation permanently.
- ## Core Concept
	- The fibre optics analogy: in the late 1990s, investors bought fibre optic companies as a proxy for internet growth. When enough fibre was in the ground and capacity became cheap, those companies collapsed even though the internet itself continued to grow. Blockspace is the same: it is infrastructure, not the application layer. Once it became abundant, cheap, and fast, the investment case for blockspace-based tokens (L1/L2 governance tokens) effectively disappeared. The internet analogy implies the applications built on top not the infrastructure itself are where value will accrue.
- ## Mechanics
	- The thesis has practical implications for portfolio construction:
		- Structurally underweight or short L1 and L2 governance tokens as a class, especially those with no path to meaningful fee revenue.
		- The "winning" chains are likely limited to Ethereum and possibly Solana; all others are effectively R&D that will eventually be absorbed or rendered irrelevant.
		- Look for protocols where there is a direct, quantifiable relationship between business performance and token value (e.g. Hyperliquid: real revenue, measurable P/E equivalent).
		- Avoid protocols where the token value rests solely on the "this chain will win" narrative.
		- When L1/L2 tokens pump in correlation with Bitcoin, recognise this as a beta bounce driven by temporary capital flows, not a fundamental re-rating. Sell into it.
- ## Examples
	- Ethereum blob launch: to approximately the day that blobs launched and blockspace became abundant, ETH and its associated alt ecosystem began a sustained de-rating. Starkiller identifies this as not coincidental.
	- Hyperliquid: counterexample. Has fee revenue, reasonable tokenomics, a direct relationship between DEX volume and token value. Earns a place in the long book.
	- Dogecoin: zero intrinsic value by any measure. Cross-sectional momentum may still trade it, but the fundamental overlay would exclude it from the long book.
- ## Caveats & Edge Cases
	- The thesis does not mean all crypto is worthless it is specifically a claim about blockspace and L1/L2 governance tokens.
	- The "R&D flows to Ethereum" prediction is a strong long-term claim with meaningful uncertainty; Solana is acknowledged as a possible exception.
	- Even structurally worthless tokens can have strong momentum signals in the short term and should be traded accordingly by the momentum strategy.
	- The thesis may be partially priced in already; the timing of when the remaining de-rating occurs is unknowable.
