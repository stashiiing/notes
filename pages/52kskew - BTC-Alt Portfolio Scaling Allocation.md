- # BTC-Alt Portfolio Scaling Allocation
- ## TL;DR
	- A ~50/50 split between Bitcoin and a basket of alts. The BTC half captures the broad upside that drives every other token. The alt half is built by scaling 1–5% of total portfolio into each name early in a trend; if alts return 2x–10x, that half contributes a 25–50x net return to the total book. Risk is managed by cutting exposure on news-driven pullbacks and either fully exiting or doubling down after re-evaluation.
- ### Context
	- BTC drives the initial leg of every crypto bull cycle but doesn't produce the generational multiples — those come from alts. Holding only BTC underperforms in cycle peaks; holding only alts gives up the foundation that the alt rally depends on. The split tries to capture both.
- ## Core Concept
	- BTC is the anchor — capture the macro crypto trend cheaply with a large allocation. Alts are the asymmetric bet — small per-name sizing, many names, scaled into early. Because alts compound off BTC strength, the structure is hierarchical: BTC works first, alts follow.
- ## Mechanics
	- **Core (50% BTC).** Captures the broad upside that initiates every crypto bull leg.
	- **Satellite (50% alts).** Selected via the sector-rotation scan. Per-name exposure is 1–5% of total portfolio early on, scaled up as the trend confirms.
	- **Concentration math.** If the alt half compounds 2x–10x across positions, that half alone can return 25x–50x on the total book.
	- **Risk-based rebalancing.** When alts approach the verge of risk (gains lost, going negative) during a news-driven pullback, cut significant exposure and re-evaluate. Decision is binary — exit completely or double down — not a slow drift.
	- **Macro overlay.** During risk-off events (war, geopolitical shocks), traditional finance dumps because TradFi hates risk and volatility. Both spike simultaneously in crypto. Those moments can become opportunities, but only if you can time the resolution.

- ## Risk Management
	- Per-name cap of 1–5% of total portfolio on scaling entries. On news-driven pullbacks: cut hard, re-evaluate, then choose between full exit or double down. Don't average passively into bleeding positions.

- ## Caveats & Edge Cases
	- "Do not do that if you are new to trading cuz it is a royal pain in the ass to manage the amount of risk associated with that. It's very degenerate." The structure assumes you can manage many positions simultaneously and react to news quickly.
	- The strategy is regime-dependent — built for early-to-mid bull trends, not for ranges or downtrends.
	- During war / macro shock periods, hedge logic matters more than allocation logic. Margin in the market becomes the primary risk variable.
