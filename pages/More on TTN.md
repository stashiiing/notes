#### What this isn't
	- Not financial advice. Everything here is educational and reflects what specific people have said. None of it is a recommendation to trade.
	- Not a ranking. Inclusion isn't endorsement. Traders disagree with each other constantly, that's part of the value. You'll find conflicting approaches side by side.
	- Not a substitute for thinking. Reading about a strategy is not the same as trading it. Test, paper-trade, fail small.
- #### How it works
	- The archive is open source. Anyone can suggest a new trader, propose a new page, correct an existing one, or flag something inaccurate. Contributions go through review to keep quality high and prevent shilling. Traders themselves are welcome to edit or comment on their own pages - if something misrepresents your view, say so and we'll fix it.
